Privacy

Your voice and route searches go to Google so I can understand you and find routes. Camera pictures stay on this phone, except one photo sent to Google when you say read or describe this. Recording a session stays on this phone.

What leaves your phone

  • Your voice, when you tap to talk, goes to Google Speech-to-Text so the app can understand you. If Google listening is off or not working, Chrome's own speech recogniser is used instead, which also sends sound to Google.
  • Place and route searches, and your location at the time of the search, go to Google (Places and Routes). If Google cannot be reached, they go to OpenStreetMap services (Nominatim and routing.openstreetmap.de) instead.
  • "Where am I?" sends your location to OpenStreetMap to find the street name.

What stays on your phone

  • Camera pictures. Detection runs inside the browser on your phone. Only when you say read or describe this is one photo sent to Google Cloud Vision, to read writing or describe the picture. Nothing else is uploaded.
  • Places near you. When you ask what is near you, your location goes to Google Places to find the shops and places around you.
  • Saved places, routes, notes and settings are kept in this browser only.
  • Recordings. "Record session" asks first, and only starts after you say yes. The video and log are saved to this phone. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose to share the files.
