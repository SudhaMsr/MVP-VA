What Vision Assist cannot see

I can't reliably see kerbs, drops, potholes, low branches, ice, or traffic signals. Keep using your cane or guide dog.

Never rely on it for

  • Crossing roads. It cannot read traffic signals or the green man. At a crossing it says: "Crossing ahead. I can't read the traffic signal. Listen for traffic before you cross."
  • Kerbs, drops and steps down. Stairs are sometimes spotted, but not reliably, and kerb edges are not.
  • Things at head height, such as branches, open van doors or scaffolding.
  • Ice, wet leaves, holes and uneven paving.
  • Quiet vehicles. Electric cars and bicycles can come close before the camera picks them up.

When it is least reliable

  • In the dark, in heavy rain, or with the camera pointing at the ground or the sky.
  • On busy pavements, where people block the view.
  • With no internet: obstacle warnings still work, but directions and Google listening do not.
  • With a weak GPS signal: turns can come late. It says so when the signal is weak.

Silence does not mean safe

When the app says nothing, it has not recognised anything worth saying. That is not the same as the way being free. Say "what can't you see" at any time to hear this list again.
