We publish how well Vision Assist works on real walks, including when it gets things wrong. No numbers are shown until they have been measured.

The bar before blind testers use it

Stage 1 gate: 0 missed moving vehicles and fewer than 1 false warning per minute, across 10 recorded walks.

How the numbers are made

A sighted tester walks with the app recording. Every warning is checked against the video and marked correct or false, and every moving vehicle the app missed is added. The scorer (tools/score_walk.mjs in the source code) adds them up.
